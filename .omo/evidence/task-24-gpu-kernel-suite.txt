GOLDEN MAPPING (documented decision): the golden harness has six families
(matmul/bias_gelu/softmax/layernorm/reduction/attention); there is NO plain
add family. The elementwise-add template validates against `reduction` with
shape 2,N: the reference y[j] = sum_i x[i,j] (axis-0 sum of a 2-row tensor)
IS x[0] + x[1] — the elementwise add of two vectors in fp32 — hence the
bit-exact verdict (cosine 1.0 / max_rel_err 0.0 / pcc 1.0).
